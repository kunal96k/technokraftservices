Enterprise Systems / Web App

Capernaum Solutions

  • Client: Capernaum Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
  • Tech Stack: Java, Spring Boot, React.js, MySQL, AWS Cloud
  • Deployment: Live in Production (AWS Cloud)
  • Impact: Centralized Enterprise Account Orchestration & Revenue Visibility
Request an Enterprise ERP Demo

Project Overview & Business Problem

Capernaum Solutions required a centralized corporate ERP to unify strategic account management, multi-department approval flows, resource tracking, and live executive revenue projections. Disconnected department spreadsheets were causing communication silos and delayed milestone sign-offs.

Key Architecture & Modules

  • Strategic Account Dashboard: Real-time status of corporate client engagements.
  • Approval Pipelines: Multi-tier authorization for department budgets and deliverables.
  • Revenue Analytics: Live milestone forecasting and financial ledger tracking.
  • AWS Cloud Hosting: High-concurrency architecture with automated backup routines.
