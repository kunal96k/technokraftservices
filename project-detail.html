Intern Management System (IMS)

Back to All Projects Back to All Projects

A comprehensive intern management and institutional portal engineered to streamline intern supervision, automate biometric and geo-tagged punch-in/punch-out time tracking, maintain Jira-style milestone records, and deliver automated evaluation reports with integrated email support.

Key Architecture & Features

  • Dual Admin & College Institutional Dashboards with role-based permissions.
  • Biometric / Geo punch-in & punch-out attendance logging with tamper prevention.
  • Jira-style task boards, sprint milestones, and daily log review workflows.
  • Automated performance scoring, transcript generation, and evaluation certificates.
  • High-concurrency AWS cloud deployment ensuring 99.9% uptime and zero latency.
